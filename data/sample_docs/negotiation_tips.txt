Salary Negotiation for Engineers 2026

The golden rule: Never accept the first offer.
Engineers who negotiate earn $5,000-$20,000 more per year on average.

When to negotiate:
- After receiving a written offer (not verbal)
- Before signing anything
- When switching jobs (easiest leverage point)
- At annual review (with competing offer if possible)

How to counter an offer:
1. Express enthusiasm first: "I'm really excited about this role"
2. Ask for time: "Can I have 48 hours to review?"
3. Research the market: Use Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, LinkedIn Salary
4. Counter 15-20% above their offer
5. Justify with market data, not personal needs

Scripts that work:
"Based on my research and experience with [specific skill], I was 
expecting something closer to [X]. Is there flexibility there?"

"I have another offer for [X]. I'd prefer to join your team — 
can you match or come closer to that?"

"I'm flexible on base if we can improve the equity/bonus structure."

What to negotiate beyond salary:
- Signing bonus (often easier to get than salary increase)
- Remote work flexibility
- Extra PTO days
- Professional development budget ($2,000-$5,000/year)
- Equipment allowance
- Earlier performance review (6 months instead of 12)

For Pakistani engineers negotiating remote roles:
- Research USD market rates, not local rates
- Emphasize timezone overlap and communication skills
- Offer a trial project to reduce employer risk perception
- Start higher than you'd accept — they will counter
