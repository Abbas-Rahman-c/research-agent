Pakistan Tech Market for Engineers 2026

Local market overview:
Pakistan's tech industry is growing at 25% annually. The IT export 
target is $5 billion by 2026, up from $2.6 billion in 2023.

Top tech companies in Pakistan:
- Systems Limited: Largest IT company, 10,000+ employees
- NetSol Technologies: Listed on NASDAQ, fintech focus
- Teradata Pakistan: Data and AI analytics
- i2c Inc: Fintech and payment systems
- 10Pearls: US-based, large Pakistan office
- Arbisoft: Product company, remote-first culture
- Tintash: Game development and mobile apps

AI/ML salaries in Pakistan (2026):
- Junior AI Engineer (0-2 years): PKR 150,000 - 250,000/month
- Mid-level AI Engineer (2-5 years): PKR 250,000 - 500,000/month
- Senior AI Engineer (5+ years): PKR 500,000 - 1,000,000/month
- Remote international: $2,000 - $8,000/month (paid in USD)

Top cities for tech jobs:
- Karachi: Financial hub, most multinational offices
- Lahore: Fastest growing tech scene, many startups
- Islamabad: Government tech projects, NUST talent pool
- Rawalpindi: Growing, close to Islamabad
